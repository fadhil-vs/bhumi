📹 How to Upload Videos

  1. Download your Instagram Reels to MP4 format using any tool you prefer.
  2. Upload the MP4 file for each production step below.
  3. Add a title in English and Malayalam.
  4. Optionally upload a thumbnail image (shown while the video loads).
  5. Toggle "Active" to show or hide each video on the site.

Videos autoplay silently and loop — no controls shown to customers.
