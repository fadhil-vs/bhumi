Our Story

More Than Spices.
A Tradition
of Flavour.

Rooted in the spice-rich lands of Kerala, Bhumi Foods was founded with a simple belief — that great food begins with honest ingredients.

From our processing unit in Mundur, Thrissur, we source, clean, dry, grind and pack every product with the same attention that home cooks bring to their kitchens.

No shortcuts. No additives. Just the pure, intense flavours that Kerala is known for.

Pure Ingredients From farms we trust, chosen with care.
Fresh Grinding Ground fresh for the fullest flavour.
Kerala Tradition Recipes rooted in generations of cooking.
